SP101 Course Outline

Elementary Spanish I · Novice Mid with guided extension toward Novice High · 5 Units · 24 Lessons + 5 Cuéntame · 4 Optional Extensions

Course Structure

Canonical public sequence approved August 10, 2026. SP101 contains five units, 24 regular lessons, and five cumulative Cuéntame capstones. Four optional Alcance Completo modules extend grammar but are not a sixth unit and are not prerequisites for the core course outcomes.

Narrative route: Mexico City/CEPE and Expo Café → Tepoztlán–Cuernavaca → Mexico City/Xochimilco → Lake Pátzcuaro–Janitzio plus monarch territory in Michoacán/State of Mexico → Veracruz/Córdoba. Each place has a distinct narrative purpose; the course does not merge regional practices into a single generic “Mexican culture.”

Setting verification: CEPE–UNAM Spanish courses · Expo Café 2026 · Tepoztlán, Morelos · Linares cartonería legacy · INAH Piedra del Sol · CONANP monarch annual cycle.

P — Productive core
8–12 high-frequency words or chunks needed for the task and useful beyond the cultural scene.
M — Mexican anchor
3–5 items that situate a place, practice, or object; productive only when immediately useful.
R — Receptive cultural richness
Regional, historical, Indigenous, ecological, or craft terms supported by image, audio, or microgloss and never required as unsupported production.

Product route: eBook introduces language in context → Compendio preserves Key Words / Vocabulary and adds Úsalo hoy cards labeled Daily life, Travel, or Both → Composición and Reto activate the same language → Cuéntame integrates it. Úsalo hoy is a transfer block, not a fifth product.

CT1 Cuéntame — Huehuetéotl: El Anciano del Fuego

Guiding question
How do greeting, register, and fire change an encounter between strangers?
Exact productive ceiling
ser for identity; hay/no hay; articles, gender and number; spelling and numbers 0–31; U1 questions and repair chunks.
Cultural nucleus
Page 1: Huehuetéotl and central Mexico: name, territory, and representation
Page 2: The brazier and fire: object, place, and symbolism
Page 3: From hearth to road: guidance, memory, and oral narrative
Language control
Zero new productive grammar. Cultural names and unsupported narrative forms are glossed; no new tense or conjugation is required.
Capstone evidence
Recover unit pieces → predict/order → listen globally → read/listen with support → reconstruct → interpret one cultural source → produce a novice-level response → ask and answer one brief question.
