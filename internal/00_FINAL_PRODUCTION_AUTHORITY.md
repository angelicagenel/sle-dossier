## 2. Canonical architecture

- 5 units, 24 regular lessons, 5 Cuéntame closers (one per unit).
- 4 objects per regular lesson: eBook, Compendio, Composición, Reto oral.
- Quizzes, objective exams, and LMS exercises are **not** built inside Rise.
- Responsible target: consistent **Novice Mid**, with guided extension
  toward Novice High. Never promise uniform Novice High.
- Productive Spanish = Mexican Spanish with `ustedes`, no `vosotros`.
  Regional voices are situated, never presented as "how all of Mexico
  speaks."

### Non-negotiable limits

- Direct object pronouns are **SP102** material. No DOP in any SP101 module,
  Can-Do statement, eBook, Compendio, Composición, Reto, or Cuéntame.
- Lexicalized chunks (`me gusta`, `lo siento`) are fine as chunks; never
  expand into the pronoun system.
- Broad `ser/estar` contrast is SP102 material; SP101 keeps functional,
  meaning-based uses only.
- Stem changes/irregulars are taught inline when the task needs them
  (`tener`, `hago`, `querer/preferir`, `pedir`, `poder`, `ir`) — never as a
  separate "Coverage Boost" track.
- Past tense, passive/impersonal `se`, reflexives, and complex historical
  language may appear only as supported receptive input inside Cuéntame —
  never as production or a grading criterion.
- Every Cuéntame adds **zero new productive grammar**.

## 6. Vocabulary rules (P/M/R) and product route

- **P — productive core:** 8–12 high-frequency words/chunks per lesson,
  useful in daily life, campus, U.S. services, or travel.
- **M — Mexican anchor:** 3–5 items that situate a place/practice/object;
  productive only if also immediately useful communicatively.
- **R — receptive richness:** historical/regional/Indigenous/ecological/craft
  vocabulary with image, audio, or microgloss support; never required as
  unsupported production.
- Product route: **eBook → Compendio → Composición → Reto → Cuéntame.** The
  Compendio keeps `Key Words / Vocabulary` and adds `Úsalo hoy` cards labeled
  `Daily life`, `Travel`, or `Both`. No fifth product, no parallel tourist
  vocabulary bank.
- Every Cuéntame needs 3 short cultural pages: (1) territory & voices,
  (2) history/story/object with evidence, (3) contemporary continuity,
  craft, or conservation.
- **CT2 control (approved):** ~70–100 words per page, short sentences, max
  6–8 distributed R terms, 45–60 second final output. The old 3-card museum
  + 75–90s audioguide format is retired — too dense for CT2's ceiling.
- Do not let source-control vocabulary (`procedencia`, `autoría`,
  `evidencia`, `atribuir`, `fusionar`, `continuidad`) dominate a beginner's
  productive core — it's an editorial tool, not student-facing vocabulary.
